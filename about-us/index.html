Reimagining Education for Every Learner

Include Education Group was founded on an unshakeable belief: education must adapt to every child's unique potential, not the other way around. We exist to prove that inclusive education works for all learners.

Through our network of innovative schools, we demonstrate how therapeutic communities create conditions where every child discovers their brilliance.

Damian Belshaw

Executive Director, Education

Two decades of transforming education for learners who don’t fit traditional systems. Damian designs evidence-based approaches that turn exclusion into engagement. His expertise in trauma-informed practice and adaptive learning creates conditions where every child discovers their potential.

Jonathan Popoola

Executive Director, Strategy & Innovation

Technology leader reimagining how education adapts to individual learners. Jonathan built GoPetey, an adaptive-learning platform, and brings digital innovation across education, health and finance. He transforms complex challenges into scalable solutions.

Our story

When Damian and Jonathan met at an Innovation in Education conference, they shared a revolutionary vision: education systems that flex to meet every learner’s unique strengths. Their combined expertise—therapeutic education and strategic innovation proves what’s possible when we design learning around children rather than expecting children to adapt to us.

Today, our growing network of schools demonstrates this philosophy in action. Each Include school creates inclusive communities where diverse learning differences become celebrated strengths. We’re not just changing individual lives—we’re proving how education should work for all children.
